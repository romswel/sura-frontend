# Sura concept image assets

Created 2026-11-17 (Asia/Manila) for the original Sura rebuild.

## Local typography

Manrope variable is stored in public/fonts/manrope-variable.ttf (164,700 bytes), with its upstream license in public/fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt. Files were obtained from the [official Google Fonts directory](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope), including its [license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt). The app serves the font locally with a system fallback and no third-party font request.

## Provenance and use

These three photographs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. They are fictional concept imagery, not photographs of Sura client deliveries, products for sale by Sura, or real commissioned architecture projects. No reference-project assets, external stock photographs, logos, or people were used. No paid API/CLI fallback or API key was used.

The original PNG outputs remain in the tool's default generated-image directory. The project uses local WebP derivatives in `public/images/`; the app has no image-hosting dependency. Derivatives were resized proportionally and encoded with Pillow at quality 84; composition and contents were not edited.

## Exact generation prompts

### offday-objects

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: original fictional furniture shop concept photograph for a web development studio portfolio demonstration. Create an elegant photorealistic editorial product photograph of one sculptural cobalt blue upholstered lounge chair with polished tubular metal legs, paired with one low cream side table, in a minimal light grey studio. Refined contemporary furniture design with believable construction, fabric texture, and soft directional studio daylight; a subtle shadow and grounded objects. Landscape 3:2 composition, objects centered with generous breathing room, perspective at seated eye level. Palette cool light grey, vivid cobalt blue, and warm cream. No people, no text, no logos, no signage, no borders, no watermarks. This is a photographic asset, not a webpage or UI mockup. Do not add any extra products.

### morrow-coffee

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original fictional neighborhood cafe concept photograph for a web development studio portfolio demonstration. Create a rich editorial close-up photograph of a small latte with distinct crema and fine foam in a clear glass on a brushed stainless steel cafe counter, with one flaky butter croissant on a simple small plate. Warm low window sunlight makes crisp highlights across the steel, with natural shadows and tactile crumbs. Contemporary independent cafe feel, believable imperfect real textures, subtle warm brown and butter yellow palette, no commercial branding. Landscape 3:2 composition at counter level, coffee and croissant centered and fully visible, background gently out of focus. No people, no text, no logos, no signage, no borders, no watermarks. This is a photographic asset, not a webpage or UI mockup.

### forma-space

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original fictional architecture studio portfolio photograph for a web development studio demonstration. Create an art-directed photograph of a quiet refined residential interior detail: one warm natural timber chair and a slender brushed stainless steel standing lamp against an offwhite plaster wall. Dramatic late afternoon window sunlight casts a distinct architectural rectangular shadow across the wall and pale stone floor. Beautiful realistic timber grain, tactile plaster, balanced proportions and credible joins; restrained architectural photography, warm neutral palette, low clutter. Landscape 3:2 composition with the chair and lamp centered, entire objects visible, generous negative space. No people, no text, no logos, no signage, no borders, no watermarks. This is a photographic asset, not a webpage or UI mockup.
